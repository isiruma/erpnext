# Implementation Roadmap

This folder contains business-driven development strategy and phased implementation approach for ERP systems, prioritizing **business value delivery** over technical features.

## Purpose

Documents the strategic approach to ERP implementation using the proven **4-Phase Business Value Delivery** model, ensuring rapid business benefits while building toward comprehensive ERP functionality.

## 4-Phase Implementation Strategy

### **Phase 1: Foundation** (Months 1-3)
- Core framework and user management
- Basic business master data setup
- **Business Value**: User access and basic data management

### **Phase 2: Core Modules** (Months 4-9) 
- CRM, Sales, Inventory, Basic Accounting
- Core business workflows (Order-to-Cash foundation)
- **Business Value**: Sales order processing and inventory tracking

### **Phase 3: Advanced Features** (Months 10-13)
- Complete Procure-to-Pay workflow
- Advanced reporting and business intelligence
- **Business Value**: Full procurement automation and business insights

### **Phase 4: Enterprise Features** (Months 14-24)
- Multi-company operations and Plan-to-Produce workflows
- Advanced integrations and customizations
- **Business Value**: Enterprise-scale operations and manufacturing

## Business-First Development Approach

- **Immediate Business Value** - Focus on core business transactions first
- **Workflow-Driven Development** - Build complete business processes, not isolated features
- **User Adoption Priority** - Ensure business user success at each phase
- **Measurable Business Impact** - Define success metrics for each delivery phase

## What Goes Here

- **Development Phases** - Business value-driven development phases
- **Technology Stack** - Business-appropriate technology decisions
- **Architecture Decisions** - Technical choices supporting business requirements
- **MVP Planning** - Minimum viable product for each business area
- **Best Practices** - Lessons learned from successful ERP implementations

## Relationship to ERP Framework

This roadmap ensures systematic business value delivery, building from core business needs toward comprehensive ERP functionality while maintaining user adoption and business benefit focus.
