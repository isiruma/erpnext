# Learning Resources

This folder contains **business-focused** educational materials that prioritize understanding ERP workflows and business value over technical implementation details.

## Purpose

Provides learning materials that emphasize **business logic, workflows, and process understanding** for stakeholders involved in ERP projects, with minimal focus on technical coding details.

## Business-First Learning Approach

Learning materials prioritize business understanding:
- **ERP Fundamentals** - Business concepts and value proposition first
- **Workflow Understanding** - Business process flows before technical implementation
- **Business Process Modeling** - Industry best practices and patterns
- **Change Management** - User adoption and business transformation success

## Learning Priorities by Role

### **Business Stakeholders** (Primary Focus)
- ERP business concepts and value proposition
- Industry workflow patterns and best practices
- Business process optimization opportunities
- ROI measurement and business case development

### **Project Managers** (Secondary Focus)
- ERP project planning and risk management
- Business value delivery strategies
- Change management and user adoption
- Success metrics and project governance

### **System Designers** (When Needed)
- Business requirements analysis techniques
- Process mapping and workflow documentation
- Business rule specification methods
- User experience design for business workflows

## What Goes Here

- **ERP Fundamentals** - Business concepts, value proposition, and industry overview
- **Business Process Modeling** - Workflow design and process optimization techniques
- **Enterprise Software Patterns** - Proven business patterns and industry practices
- **Glossary** - Business terminology and ERP concepts
- **External Resources** - Industry resources, standards, and best practices

## Relationship to ERP Framework

These resources support business understanding and successful ERP adoption, focusing on business value delivery rather than technical implementation complexity.
