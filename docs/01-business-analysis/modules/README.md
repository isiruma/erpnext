# Modules

This folder contains **business-focused analysis** of individual ERP modules, emphasizing their **business functions and workflow contributions** to the core ERP processes.

## Purpose

Documents how each ERP module supports business operations and contributes to the **Order-to-Cash**, **Procure-to-Pay**, and **Plan-to-Produce** workflows. Focus is on business value delivery and cross-module business process integration.

## Business Module Categories

### **Core Business Modules**
Modules that directly support primary business workflows:
- **Accounts Module** - Financial management across all business workflows
- **Sales Module** - Order-to-Cash process execution and customer management
- **Purchasing Module** - Procure-to-Pay process execution and supplier management
- **Inventory Module** - Stock management supporting all business workflows

### **Supporting Business Modules**
Modules that enhance and extend core business capabilities:
- **Manufacturing Module** - Plan-to-Produce workflow execution
- **CRM Module** - Customer relationship management supporting sales
- **Projects Module** - Project-based business operations and resource management
- **Assets Module** - Asset lifecycle management and maintenance

## Business Integration Focus

Each module analysis emphasizes:
- **Business Workflow Contribution** - How the module supports core ERP processes
- **Cross-Module Business Logic** - Business rules that span multiple modules
- **Business Data Flow** - How business information moves between modules
- **Business Value Delivery** - Specific business benefits and outcomes

## Module Business Relationships

- **Accounts ↔ Sales** - Revenue recognition and customer billing
- **Accounts ↔ Purchasing** - Expense management and vendor payments
- **Inventory ↔ Sales** - Order fulfillment and stock allocation
- **Inventory ↔ Purchasing** - Stock replenishment and goods receipt
- **Manufacturing ↔ Inventory** - Material consumption and production output

## What Goes Here

- **Accounts Module** - Financial management and double-entry accounting
- **Sales Module** - Customer management and Order-to-Cash execution
- **Purchasing Module** - Supplier management and Procure-to-Pay execution
- **Inventory Module** - Stock management and warehouse operations
- **Manufacturing Module** - Production planning and Plan-to-Produce execution
- **CRM Module** - Customer relationship management and lead conversion
- **Projects Module** - Project management and resource allocation
- **Assets Module** - Asset lifecycle management and maintenance tracking

## Relationship to ERP Framework

These module analyses ensure cohesive business functionality while maintaining the document-centric, event-driven architecture that supports integrated business workflows.
