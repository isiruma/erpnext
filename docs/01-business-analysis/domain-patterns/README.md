# Domain Patterns

This folder contains **industry-specific business patterns** that adapt the core ERP workflows (Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, Plan-to-Produce) to different business contexts and industry requirements.

## Purpose

Documents how core ERP business logic adapts to different industries and business models, focusing on **business workflow variations** rather than technical customizations. Each pattern shows how universal business processes apply to specific industry contexts.

## Industry Business Model Focus

Domain patterns show how core business workflows adapt to industry needs:
- **Manufacturing Business** - Production-focused operations with Plan-to-Produce emphasis
- **Retail Business** - Sales and inventory-focused with Order-to-Cash optimization
- **Services Business** - Project and time-based operations with resource management

## Core Business Adaptations

### **Manufacturing-Focused Operations**
- Plan-to-Produce workflow with production planning and material requirements
- Quality control integration throughout manufacturing processes
- Cost accounting for production and work-in-progress tracking
- Equipment maintenance and capacity planning

### **Retail-Focused Operations**
- Order-to-Cash optimization for high-volume, low-margin transactions
- Multi-channel sales integration (online, in-store, mobile)
- Inventory optimization with demand forecasting
- Point-of-sale integration and customer loyalty programs

### **Services-Focused Operations**
- Project-based Order-to-Cash with time and material billing
- Resource allocation and project profitability tracking
- Professional services automation and client management
- Contract management and recurring billing patterns

## Business Model Variations

- **B2B Operations** - Long sales cycles, complex approvals, contract-based pricing
- **B2C Operations** - High-volume transactions, simplified processes, customer self-service
- **Subscription Models** - Recurring revenue, customer lifecycle management, usage tracking
- **Multi-Channel** - Integrated operations across sales channels and touchpoints

## What Goes Here

- **Manufacturing Business** - Production-focused business workflow patterns
- **Retail Business** - Sales and inventory-optimized business patterns
- **Services Business** - Project and time-based business operation patterns

## Relationship to ERP Framework

These patterns demonstrate how the document-centric, event-driven ERP framework adapts to different business contexts while maintaining core workflow integrity.
