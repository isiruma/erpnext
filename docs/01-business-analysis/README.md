# Business Analysis

This section focuses on understanding ERP business logic, workflows, and requirements - the foundation for building effective enterprise software.

## 📋 Contents Overview

### 🔄 [Core Workflows](./workflows/)

- **Order-to-Cash** - Complete sales cycle from lead to payment
- **Procure-to-Pay** - Purchase cycle from requirement to payment
- **Plan-to-Produce** - Manufacturing cycle from planning to delivery
- **Supporting Processes** - Inventory, financial, and administrative workflows

Core business domains and their functionality:
- **Accounts Module** - Financial management and compliance

- **Inventory Module** - Stock management and warehousing
- **Manufacturing Module** - Production planning and execution
- **CRM Module** - Customer relationship management
- **Projects Module** - Project delivery and management
- **Assets Module** - Fixed asset lifecycle management

- **Manufacturing Business** - Production-focused operations

## 🎯 Key Business Insights

### Essential Business Principles
- **Document-Based Processes** - Mirror real-world business documents
- **Status-Driven Workflows** - Clear business milestones and progression
- **Event-Driven Automation** - Automated responses to business events
- **Multi-Company Support** - Group organization scenarios

### Critical Business Logic Patterns
- **Percentage Completion Tracking** - Transparency in business processes
- **Three-Way Matching** - Procurement control (PO → Receipt → Invoice)
- **Credit Limit Management** - Financial risk control
- **Real-Time Business Data** - Informed decision making

### Process Automation Opportunities
- **Reorder Point Management** - Inventory optimization
- **Payment Reminders** - Cash flow management
- **Recurring Transactions** - Subscription business support
- **Quality Control Workflows** - Product quality assurance

## 🚀 Business Value Delivery

### Immediate Value (Months 1-3)
- Customer and product master data management
- Basic sales order and invoice processing
- Inventory tracking and stock management
- Financial transaction recording

### Medium-term Value (Months 4-9)
- Complete order-to-cash automation
- Procurement workflow optimization
- Financial reporting and compliance
- Customer relationship management

### Long-term Value (Months 10+)
- Manufacturing planning and execution
- Project management and profitability
- Business intelligence and analytics
- Multi-company and international operations
