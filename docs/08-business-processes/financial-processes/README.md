# Financial Processes

This folder contains detailed documentation for **double-entry accounting** and financial workflows that support all ERP business processes with **multi-currency** and compliance capabilities.

## Purpose

Documents financial workflows that provide the **accounting foundation** for Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, and Plan-to-Produce processes, ensuring accurate financial reporting and regulatory compliance.

## Financial Business Value

Financial processes ensure business control and compliance:
- **Cash Flow Management** - Accurate tracking of money in and money out
- **Profitability Analysis** - Revenue and cost tracking by business segment
- **Compliance Assurance** - Regulatory reporting and audit trail maintenance
- **Business Performance Measurement** - Financial KPIs and variance analysis

## Core Financial Workflows

### **Double-Entry Accounting Foundation**
- Every business transaction creates balanced journal entries
- Real-time financial position and performance tracking
- Audit trail for all financial transactions and adjustments

### **Multi-Currency Operations**
- International business transaction support
- Exchange rate management and revaluation
- Consolidated reporting across currencies

## What Goes Here

- **Accounting Basics** - Double-entry accounting principles and chart of accounts
- **Multi-Currency Operations** - International business financial management
- **Financial Reporting** - Standard reports and regulatory compliance

## Financial Integration with Business Workflows

### **Order-to-Cash Financial Impact**
- Revenue recognition from sales orders and deliveries
- Accounts receivable management and collections
- Customer credit management and payment terms

### **Procure-to-Pay Financial Impact**
- Expense recognition from purchase orders and receipts
- Accounts payable management and vendor payments
- Budget control and purchase approval workflows

### **Plan-to-Produce Financial Impact**
- Work-in-progress and finished goods valuation
- Manufacturing cost allocation and absorption
- Inventory valuation and cost of goods sold

## Business Process Integration

- **Sales Processes** - Revenue recognition and customer billing
- **Purchase Processes** - Expense management and vendor payments
- **Inventory Processes** - Stock valuation and cost of goods sold
- **Manufacturing Processes** - Production costing and work-in-progress
