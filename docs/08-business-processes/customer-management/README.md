# Customer Management

This folder contains detailed documentation for **customer relationship management (CRM) processes** that support the **Order-to-Cash workflow** and customer lifecycle management.

## Purpose

Documents the complete **Lead-to-Customer-to-Revenue** business process, focusing on relationship management activities that drive business value and customer retention throughout the Order-to-Cash workflow.

## Business Value Focus

Customer management processes drive business outcomes:
- **Revenue Growth** - Lead conversion and customer acquisition strategies
- **Customer Lifetime Value** - Retention and upselling optimization
- **Business Relationship Quality** - Communication and satisfaction management
- **Sales Pipeline Efficiency** - Opportunity management and conversion tracking

## What Goes Here

- **Lead-to-Customer Process** - Complete lead qualification and conversion workflow
- **Customer Onboarding** - New customer setup and relationship establishment
- **Customer Lifecycle Management** - Ongoing relationship and retention strategies
- **Communication Management** - Customer touchpoint coordination and follow-up

## Order-to-Cash Integration

Customer management supports the complete Order-to-Cash workflow:
- **Lead → Opportunity** - Prospect identification and qualification
- **Opportunity → Quotation** - Sales process initiation and pricing
- **Customer → Sales Order** - Order processing and fulfillment
- **Customer → Payment** - Billing, collections, and relationship maintenance

## Business Process Integration

- **Sales Processes** - Order processing and revenue generation
- **Financial Processes** - Customer billing, credit management, and collections
- **Support Processes** - Issue resolution and customer satisfaction
- **Marketing Processes** - Lead generation and customer communication
