# Inventory Processes

This folder contains detailed documentation for **stock management** and **warehouse operations** that support all business workflows with accurate inventory tracking and valuation.

## Purpose

Documents inventory workflows that provide **stock visibility and control** across Order-to-Cash, Procure-to-Pay, and Plan-to-Produce processes, ensuring accurate stock levels and efficient warehouse operations.

## Inventory Business Value

Inventory processes optimize working capital and operations:
- **Working Capital Optimization** - Right inventory levels to minimize carrying costs
- **Customer Service Excellence** - Stock availability for order fulfillment
- **Cost Control** - Accurate inventory valuation and loss prevention
- **Operational Efficiency** - Streamlined warehouse and logistics operations

## Core Inventory Workflows

### **Stock Management**
- Real-time inventory tracking and valuation
- Multiple warehouse and location management
- Batch and serial number tracking for traceability

### **Warehouse Operations**
- Receiving and put-away optimization
- Picking and shipping efficiency
- Cycle counting and stock accuracy

## What Goes Here

- **Stock Management** - Inventory tracking, valuation, and control procedures
- **Warehouse Operations** - Physical warehouse processes and optimization
- **Valuation Methods** - Inventory costing approaches and financial impact

## Inventory Integration with Business Workflows

### **Order-to-Cash Inventory Impact**
- Stock allocation and reservation for sales orders
- Order fulfillment and shipping processes
- Customer delivery and stock reduction

### **Procure-to-Pay Inventory Impact**
- Goods receipt and stock increases from purchases
- Quality control and acceptance procedures
- Supplier returns and stock adjustments

### **Plan-to-Produce Inventory Impact**
- Material consumption and work-in-progress tracking
- Finished goods production and stock increases
- Sub-assembly and component management

## Business Process Integration

- **Purchase Processes** - Goods receipt and stock replenishment
- **Sales Processes** - Order fulfillment and stock allocation
- **Manufacturing Processes** - Material consumption and production output
- **Financial Processes** - Inventory valuation and cost of goods sold
