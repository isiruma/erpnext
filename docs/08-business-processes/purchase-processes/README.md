# Purchase Processes

This folder contains detailed documentation for the complete **Procure-to-Pay workflow** including supplier management, **three-way matching**, and procurement control processes.

## Purpose

Documents the end-to-end **Procure-to-Pay business workflow** from material requirements through supplier payments, emphasizing cost control, supplier relationships, and procurement efficiency.

## Procure-to-Pay Business Value

Purchase processes control costs and ensure supply continuity:
- **Cost Control** - Competitive pricing and budget management
- **Supply Assurance** - Reliable supplier relationships and delivery
- **Quality Management** - Vendor performance and goods inspection
- **Financial Control** - Purchase approvals and three-way matching

## Complete Procure-to-Pay Workflow

**Material Request → RFQ → Purchase Order → Receipt → Invoice → Payment**

### **Three-Way Matching Control**
- **Purchase Order** - Authorization and pricing agreement
- **Goods Receipt** - Physical delivery confirmation
- **Vendor Invoice** - Payment authorization only after matching

## What Goes Here

- **Vendor Management** - Supplier relationships and performance tracking
- **Procurement Process** - Complete purchase requisition to receipt workflow
- **Supplier Payments** - Invoice processing and payment management
