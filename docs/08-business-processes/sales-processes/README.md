# Sales Processes

This folder contains detailed documentation for the complete **Order-to-Cash workflow** including sales management, quotation processes, and **quote-to-cash** execution.

## Purpose

Documents the end-to-end **Order-to-Cash business workflow** from lead generation through payment collection, emphasizing revenue generation, customer satisfaction, and sales efficiency.

## Order-to-Cash Business Value

Sales processes drive revenue and customer satisfaction:
- **Revenue Growth** - Efficient lead conversion and order processing
- **Customer Satisfaction** - Accurate order fulfillment and delivery
- **Sales Efficiency** - Streamlined quote-to-cash processes
- **Profitability Optimization** - Pricing management and margin control

## Complete Order-to-Cash Workflow

**Lead → Opportunity → Quotation → Sales Order → Delivery → Invoice → Payment**

### **Quote-to-Cash Execution**
- **Quotation** - Pricing and terms proposal
- **Sales Order** - Customer commitment and fulfillment authorization
- **Delivery** - Physical goods delivery or service completion
- **Invoice** - Billing and payment request
- **Payment** - Revenue collection and cash flow

## What Goes Here

- **Quotation Process** - Quote creation, pricing, and customer approval
- **Order Processing** - Sales order fulfillment and delivery management
- **Billing Process** - Invoicing, collections, and payment processing

## Order-to-Cash Integration with Business Workflows

### **Customer Relationship Integration**
- Lead qualification and opportunity management
- Customer communication and relationship tracking
- Customer satisfaction and retention activities

### **Inventory and Fulfillment Integration**
- Stock allocation and reservation for sales orders
- Order picking, packing, and shipping processes
- Delivery confirmation and customer acceptance

### **Financial Integration**
- Revenue recognition and accounts receivable
- Customer billing and payment collection
- Credit management and payment terms enforcement

## Business Process Integration

- **Customer Management** - Lead generation and relationship tracking
- **Inventory Processes** - Order fulfillment and stock allocation
- **Financial Processes** - Revenue recognition and customer billing
- **Manufacturing Processes** - Make-to-order production and delivery
