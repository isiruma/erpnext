# User Experience

This folder contains UI/UX patterns and design systems that make complex business workflows simple and efficient for end users.

## Purpose

Documents user experience patterns that support business productivity, emphasizing **Progressive Disclosure** and business process efficiency. The focus is on translating complex business logic into intuitive user interfaces.

## Business-Driven UX Principles

User experience design should enhance business workflow efficiency:
- **Progressive Disclosure** - Complex business processes revealed step-by-step
- **Workspace Patterns** - Business function-focused user interfaces
- **Form Design** - Business document creation and editing patterns
- **Navigation Architecture** - Business process-driven information architecture

## Core Business Workflows Supported

- **Order-to-Cash UX** - Customer journey from lead to payment
- **Procure-to-Pay UX** - Supplier workflow from requisition to payment
- **Plan-to-Produce UX** - Manufacturing workflow user experience
- **Business Intelligence UX** - Dashboard and reporting interfaces

## What Goes Here

- **Design Principles** - Business-focused design philosophy and guidelines
- **Workspace Patterns** - Role-based business function interfaces
- **Form Design** - Business document creation and editing patterns
- **Navigation Architecture** - Business process-driven site structure
- **Mobile Responsiveness** - Business workflow support on mobile devices
- **Accessibility Guidelines** - Inclusive design for business users

## Relationship to ERP Framework

This documentation ensures that business complexity is hidden behind intuitive interfaces, enabling users to focus on business value rather than system complexity.
