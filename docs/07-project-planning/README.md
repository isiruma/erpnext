This section provides templates and guidance for planning your specific ERP project, helping you organize requirements, prioritize features, and manage implementation effectively.

### [Project Requirements](./project-requirements.md)
**Define your specific business requirements**
- Functional requirements by business area
- Non-functional requirements (performance, security, scalability)
- Integration requirements with existing systems
- Compliance and regulatory requirements

### [Feature Prioritization](./feature-prioritization.md)
**Prioritize features based on business value**
- Feature impact and effort analysis
- Business value scoring methodology
- MVP feature selection criteria
- Release planning and roadmap

### [Timeline Planning](./timeline-planning.md)
**Create realistic project timelines**
- Development phase breakdown
- Milestone definition and tracking
- Dependency management
- Risk mitigation planning

### [Resource Allocation](./resource-allocation.md)
**Plan team structure and resource needs**
- Team roles and responsibilities
- Skill requirements and training needs
- External resource planning
- Budget allocation and management

### [Risk Management](./risk-management.md)
**Identify and mitigate project risks**
- Technical risk assessment
- Business risk evaluation
- Mitigation strategies and contingency plans
- Risk monitoring and reporting

## 🎯 Project Planning Methodology

### 1. Requirements Gathering
**Business-First Approach**
- Start with business process mapping
- Identify pain points in current processes
- Define success criteria and KPIs
- Engage stakeholders from all business areas

### 2. Solution Design
**Based on ERPNext Patterns**
- Use proven business workflow patterns
- Adapt standard processes to your needs
- Design for scalability and growth
- Plan for future enhancement needs

### 3. Implementation Strategy
**Phased Delivery Approach**
- **Phase 1**: Foundation and core modules
- **Phase 2**: Advanced features and integrations
- **Phase 3**: Optimization and analytics
- **Phase 4**: Advanced automation and AI

### 4. Change Management
**User Adoption Focus**
- Training program development
- Change communication strategy
- User feedback and iteration
- Success measurement and improvement

## 📊 Project Planning Templates

### Business Case Template
```
Project Overview:
- Business objectives and goals
- Current state analysis and pain points
- Proposed solution benefits
- Success metrics and KPIs

Investment Analysis:
- Development costs and timeline
- Infrastructure and licensing costs
- Training and change management costs
- Expected ROI and payback period

Risk Assessment:
- Technical risks and mitigation
- Business risks and contingency plans
- Resource risks and alternatives
- Timeline risks and buffers
```

### Requirements Documentation Template
```
Functional Requirements:
- Business process requirements
- User interface requirements
- Integration requirements
- Reporting and analytics requirements

Non-Functional Requirements:
- Performance requirements
- Security requirements
- Scalability requirements
- Availability requirements

Acceptance Criteria:
- Business acceptance criteria
- Technical acceptance criteria
- Performance acceptance criteria
- Security acceptance criteria
```

### Project Charter Template
```
Project Scope:
- In-scope business areas and processes
- Out-of-scope items and exclusions
- Integration boundaries and interfaces
- Success criteria and deliverables

Team Structure:
- Project sponsor and steering committee
- Project manager and technical lead
- Business analysts and subject matter experts
- Development team and QA resources

Timeline and Budget:
- High-level project timeline
- Budget allocation by phase
- Resource allocation plan
- Key milestones and deliverables
```

## 🏗️ Implementation Approach

### Agile Development
**Iterative delivery with business feedback**
- 2-4 week sprint cycles
- Regular business user demos
- Continuous feedback and adjustment
- Early and frequent value delivery

### Business-Driven Prioritization
**Focus on business value and impact**
- Core business processes first
- High-impact, low-effort features
- User feedback driving priorities
- Continuous business value assessment

### Risk-Managed Delivery
**Proactive risk identification and mitigation**
- Technical proof of concepts early
- Integration testing throughout
- Performance testing from start
- Security validation at each phase

## 📈 Success Metrics

### Business Metrics
- **Process Efficiency** - Time reduction in key processes
- **Data Accuracy** - Reduction in data errors and corrections
- **User Productivity** - Tasks completed per user per day
- **Customer Satisfaction** - Internal and external customer feedback

### Technical Metrics
- **System Performance** - Response times and throughput
- **System Availability** - Uptime and reliability metrics
- **Data Quality** - Completeness, accuracy, and consistency
- **Security Compliance** - Vulnerability assessments and audits

### Project Metrics
- **Timeline Adherence** - Milestones delivered on schedule
- **Budget Performance** - Actual vs. planned costs
- **Scope Management** - Changes and their impact
- **Quality Metrics** - Defect rates and user acceptance
